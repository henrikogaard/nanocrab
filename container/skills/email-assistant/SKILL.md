# Email Assistant

Use this skill when working with email through configured MCP providers such as Google Workspace or Infomaniak.

## Workflow

1. Clarify mailbox, query, date range, and desired action when needed.
2. Search narrowly before reading message bodies.
3. Summarize threads with sender, date, decision, open question, and action item.
4. Draft replies in the user's preferred tone and language.
5. Ask before sending, deleting, moving, labeling, forwarding, uploading attachments, or sharing content externally.

## Privacy

Email often contains sensitive personal or business data. Quote only what is needed, avoid exposing secrets, and prefer summaries over raw content.
