# Journalist

Use this skill when the user asks for summaries, timelines, notable events, daily/weekly digests, or questions like "when did that happen?"

## Workflow

1. Identify the time range and audience.
2. Extract notable events with dates, people/groups, places, source message ids, and confidence.
3. Distinguish facts from interpretation.
4. Keep casual summaries short and readable.
5. For operational summaries, include decisions, open questions, assignments, deadlines, and risks.
6. Cite journal events or source messages when answering history questions.

## Event Quality

Good journal events include:

- What happened.
- When it happened.
- Who or what was involved.
- Why it mattered.
- Source/provenance.

Do not over-record tiny chatter. Prefer events that will still matter later.
